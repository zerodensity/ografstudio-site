Each guide takes one task from start to finish. They follow the same version as the
[documentation](/docs), which has the complete reference.

## Getting started

- [Install and run OGraf Studio](/guides/install): download the single-file server, or run from
  source.
- [Make your first lower third](/guides/first-lower-third): start from a broadcast recipe, edit it,
  preview it and save it.

## Building graphics

- [Animate with steps and keyframes](/guides/animate): the OGraf lifecycle, the timeline and
  keyframes.
- [Connect graphics to operator data](/guides/bind-data): add data fields and bind text, images and
  colours to them.
- [Add images, SVG and Lottie](/guides/images-svg-lottie): bring artwork in and keep it editable.
- [Create a shader background](/guides/shader-background): GLSL fills with operator controls.
- [Make your first chart](/guides/first-chart): an editable bar chart from a blank template, the
  other chart types, and a chart fed from a data file.

## Delivering graphics

- [Export and test an OGraf package](/guides/export): produce a checked `.ograf.zip` and test it in
  your player.
- [Share projects from GitHub](/guides/share-from-github): open `.ogs` projects straight from a URL.

## Going further

The [user guide](/docs/user-guide) covers these in full:

- [Transitions](/docs/user-guide/transitions/overview): how the templates of a set go on and off air together.
- [Visual rules](/docs/user-guide/data/visual-rules): graphics that respond to data, clicks, taps and hover.
- [Charts](/docs/user-guide/layers/charts): Chart.js layers driven by your data.
- [Media Cues](/docs/user-guide/media/media-cues): clips and live sources inside a graphic.
- [Plugins](/docs/user-guide/plugins/plugins-pane): add [shaders](/docs/shader-plugins),
  [animations](/docs/animation-plugins) and [renderers](/docs/renderer-plugins).

## Working with AI

- [Connect an AI assistant](/guides/ai-assistant): use the built-in AI Assistant, or drive Studio from
  an MCP client such as Claude or Codex.
