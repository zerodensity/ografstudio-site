A compact two-team score bug for live sport. Change a score in the form and the graphic updates
while it stays on air.

## Try it

Above the canvas, open **+ Recipe…** and choose **Scoreboard**.

## What you get

- **Layers:** a striped shader panel, the team names, both scores and a divider, all ordinary
  editable layers.
- **Actions:** Home +1 and Away +1 change the score inside the graphic; the match clock has its own
  Start, Pause, Reset and Restart.
- **Rules:** the leading score turns gold by comparing the two score fields.
- **Fields:** team names, scores, team colours and period.
- **Motion:** an entrance and an exit you can retime on the timeline.

## Make it yours

- Add a **Color** field per team and bind each side of the panel to it.
- Add a **Clock / Timer** recipe for match time.
- Test your longest team names with **Run broadcast QA** in **Preview & Export** before match day.
