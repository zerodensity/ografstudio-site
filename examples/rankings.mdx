A results board for a song contest or game show. Twelve contestants wait on the board and five
juries give their points in turn. Each time a score lands, the graphic adds it up, works out the new
ranking and slides every row to its place. Gold, silver and bronze follow whoever holds the top
three places.

## Try it

The board runs a whole vote by itself as the page opens: five juries, then the winner, and round
again. Press any button or the picture and it stops there, and the controls are yours. **Autoplay**
starts it again from a clear board.

To run the vote yourself, press **Start over**, then:

1. Press **Next vote**. Lisbon's low scores land by themselves, one a second.
2. Press **Next vote** again for the top score. The tile holds the moment, then gives the name.
3. Carry on through the five juries. The last top score decides the winner.

There's more to press along the way:

- **Next vote** while scores are still landing lands the rest at once.
- **Low scores** sets how they arrive: running by themselves, landing together, or one per press.
- **Give points** adds a score by hand, using **Give to** and **Points**.
- **Start over** clears the board. **Take out** and **Replay** keep the scores.

## What you get

- **Function:** one function, Jury vote, runs the whole show inside the graphic. It reads the
  contestants and juries, keeps the score, and writes what the layers display.
- **Runtime collections:** the board, the jury's list of scores and the jury markers are each one row
  designed once. The function gives each board row a place, and the row slides there.
- **Rules:** gold, silver and bronze are rules on a row's rank, so a tie shares its colour. The score
  chips, arrows and highlights are rules too.
- **Fields:** the title, how low scores arrive and the points scale, plus the `contestants` and
  `juries` arrays a controller or feed can send.
- **Actions:** Next vote, Give points and Start over. A controller can send Give points a payload of
  `{to, points}`, so a live phone vote can feed the same board.
- **Motion:** rows take the composition's update transition time to move, 0.64 seconds here. A row
  that climbs passes over the others.

## How the score works

- A jury's `ranking` lists contestant IDs, best first. The best gets the top of the points scale, the
  next gets the one below, and so on.
- Rows sort by points. On equal points, whoever got there first stays ahead, and both show the same
  place.
- Arrows show how far a contestant has moved since the current jury began. A contestant with no points
  has no place and no arrow.

## Make it yours

- Send your own `contestants` and `juries`. This board fits twelve contestants.
- Change **Points scale**. For a small panel, `1,2,3` works as well as the full ten scores.
- Give a contestant starting points to carry a score over from an earlier round.
- Change the pace in the numbers at the top of the function, such as the wait before a top score.
