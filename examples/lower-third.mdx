The strap most news and factual programmes use to name a speaker or a story.

## Try it

Above the canvas, open **+ Recipe…** and choose **Lower Third**. The
[first lower third guide](/guides/first-lower-third) walks through editing, previewing and saving it.

## What you get

- **Layers:** a panel, a texture, an accent bar, and the headline and subheadline text. They are
  ordinary layers in one group, so every one of them stays editable.
- **Fields:** name, role, style and live tag. One Style field maps to the accent, the tag colour and
  the tag text.
- **Motion:** an entrance into the on-air Step and an exit after it, as ordinary keyframes.

## Make it yours

- Change the panel colours in the form above, then do the same in Studio's **Properties**.
- Apply a style pack (News, Sports, Entertainment or Documentary) to set colour, type and motion
  conventions in one go. The pack copies normal Brand Kit tokens you can keep editing.
- Use **Properties → Animate In / Out** to pick another entrance, such as Wipe or Slide.
