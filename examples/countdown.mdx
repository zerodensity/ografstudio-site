A countdown for quiz rounds, breaks and live events. It starts when the graphic is taken in, warns
in the last five seconds and shows when time is up. Use **Replay** to run it again.

## Try it

Above the canvas, open **+ Recipe…** and choose **Countdown**.

## What you get

- **Layers:** a panel, a label, the timer, and the five-second warning and time-up messages.
- **Timer:** a Clock / Timer element counting down from 30 seconds in `mm:ss`. It starts
  automatically when the graphic goes on air and pauses while it's off air.
- **Alarms:** visual rules switch to the warning at five seconds and to the time-up state at zero.
- **Actions:** Start, Pause, Reset and Restart, published in the manifest for any controller.
- **Alarms:** a rule fires at five seconds and another at zero, with no controller involved.
- **Fields:** title, duration, overtime, warning on or off, and warning colour.

## Make it yours

- Change the start time and format in **Properties → Clock / Timer**.
- Edit or add alarms there too; **Edit rule** opens each alarm's rule in **Rules**.
- Switch the source to **Manual** to drive the remaining time from a data field instead.
