A time display for news and live programmes. The clock above is running in your browser, so it
shows your own local time.

## Try it

Above the canvas, open **+ Recipe…** and choose **Clock / Timer**.

## What you get

- **Layers:** a panel with a label, a digit window and the time.
- **Clock:** a Clock / Timer element showing local time in 24-hour `HH:MM:SS`.
- **Fields:** time zone, hour format and show seconds. Each one is bound to the running clock, so it
  changes on air without a restart, and the time zone also sets the label.

## Make it yours

- Change the format, time zone or hour cycle in **Properties → Clock / Timer**.
- Switch the mode to count up or count down for a timer; see the [countdown](/examples/countdown).
- Place it under the [bug](/examples/bug); the recipes are laid out to stack in the same corner.
