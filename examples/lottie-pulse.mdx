A blue circle that grows and shrinks every two seconds, using only features Studio supports. Use it
to check that Lottie layers play in your renderer before you bring in bigger animations.

## Try it

1. Download [pulse.json](pathname:///downloads/pulse.json).
2. Choose **+ Lottie JSON** above the canvas and pick the file.
3. Preview it in **Preview & Export**, then export and play it in your renderer.

## What to check

- It loops continuously while on air. Change its speed in **Properties**.
- Seeking in non-realtime preview always lands on the same frame.
- Export Lottie files from a current Bodymovin or LottieFiles version. Very old files whose keyframes
  have no easing handles can render as a solid block instead of the animation.
- Your own Lottie files should embed images as data URIs and use alpha mattes; expressions, luma
  mattes and external files aren't supported. See the [user guide](/docs/user-guide/layers/lottie).
