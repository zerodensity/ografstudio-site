A news crawl: a fixed label and a line of headlines moving across the bottom of the screen. Type
new headlines into the form and the ticker picks them up.

## Try it

Above the canvas, open **+ Recipe…** and choose **Ticker**.

## What you get

- **Layers:** a window, a label plate with its label, and a native ticker element beside it.
- **Fields:** label, headlines, scroll speed, direction, text direction, when updates apply, and a
  Breaking switch that a rule turns into a new label.
- **Motion:** the native ticker measures the current text and repeats it at a steady speed, so it
  keeps moving smoothly however long the graphic stays on air and however long the text gets.

## Make it yours

- Adjust the scroll speed and the gap between repeats in the ticker's **Properties**.
- Restyle the label plate with your Brand Kit's accent colour.
- Keep each item short enough to read at your scroll speed; check it in fullscreen preview.
