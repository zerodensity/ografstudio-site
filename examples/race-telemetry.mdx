Monza, 6 September 2020. Pierre Gasly starts the last lap of the Italian Grand Prix nine tenths of a
second ahead of Carlos Sainz, who has DRS, the tow, and one lap to use them. This graphic plays that
lap as it happened, in real time.

**There is no footage in it.** Every car's position, speed, gear, throttle, brake and DRS come from
the race's public telemetry, about nine samples a second for each of the sixteen cars still running.
The graphic redraws the lap from those numbers: where each car is on the circuit, what the two
leaders' instruments read, and how far apart they are.

This is an unofficial replay for showing what OGraf Studio can do. It isn't affiliated with
Formula 1, the FIA or any team, and it uses none of their logos or pictures.

## Try it

The player runs the lap by itself as the page opens, and starts it again a few seconds after the
result. Press any button or the picture and it stops going round, and the controls are yours.
**Autoplay** starts it again from the top.

1. Watch the gap. Sainz opens his DRS on the main straight and reaches 348 km/h against Gasly's 321,
   and is a third of a second behind into the first chicane.
2. Untick **Captions** while it plays. The box over the map goes, and nothing else is disturbed.
3. Type a new **Title**. It changes in place.
4. Press **Take out** at any moment. Everything leaves together, mid-lap or not. **Take in** plays
   the lap from the start.

## What you see

- **The track map:** the circuit's line is coloured by the leader's speed, from blue in the chicanes
  to red on the straights. Each car is a dot in its team's colour. The dotted green lines are where
  Sainz had DRS.
- **The timing tower:** every car's gap to the leader. As on a timing screen, a gap changes when its
  car crosses a timing line. Two cars are passed on this lap, and their rows change places.
- **The fight for the win:** each leader's speed, gear, revs, throttle and brake, and the gap between
  them, which is measured from their positions and agrees with the official timing at every timing
  line.
- **The speed trace:** both cars' speed through the lap, drawn as it happens. The dips are the
  corners.
- **The finish:** the flag, the winner by 0.415 seconds, and the first six with the official times.

## What you get

- **Clips for everything that moves:** each moving layer has one clip as long as the lap, which
  starts when the graphic is on air and holds its last frame. Car positions, rev rings, pedals, the
  charts being drawn and the captions are keys on those clips, about 11,500 of them, placed by a
  script from the telemetry.
- **Rolling numbers:** the speeds, the gears and the gap are strips of digits. Each strip moves
  behind a window that shows one digit, so the numbers change with the picture, frame for frame.
- **A timer with alarms:** a hidden timer starts with the clips. Rules on it set the text that
  changes now and then, 96 times in the lap: the tower's gaps, the sector times, the captions.
- **A lap clock:** the leader's lap time is a timer layer. The alarm at the line sends it back to
  zero, and it stops at the flag on 1:25.783.
- **Rev rings from a gradient:** each ring is one ellipse with a conic gradient. The clip moves two
  of its stops.
- **Pictures for what never changes:** the panels, the circuit and the fixed labels are one picture,
  drawn once with the template's own fonts. A graphic works on every layer on every frame, so the
  fewer layers, the more smoothly it plays.
- **Stop at any time:** the lap isn't a step. A step has to finish before the graphic can be taken
  out, and this one would take 103 seconds.
- **Data updates:** the title and the captions change in place, mid-lap.
- **No function:** nothing in it needs one, so there is nothing to wait for and nothing to fail.

## Where the data comes from

| What | From |
| --- | --- |
| Positions, speed, gear, revs, throttle, brake and DRS of every car, and the times at each timing line | [TracingInsights](https://tracinginsights.com/), from their [2020 season files](https://github.com/TracingInsights/2020), under the MIT licence |
| Finishing times and grid places | Their [RaceData](https://github.com/TracingInsights/RaceData) tables, CC0 |

The telemetry ends when a car takes the flag. After that the car coasts on round the circuit so the
map doesn't freeze, and its gauges hold what they read at the line.

## Make it yours

The scripts in the site's
[`examples-src`](https://github.com/zerodensity/ografstudio-site/tree/main/examples-src) folder
build it, and its README says how. To replay another race, change the race in
`race-telemetry-snapshot.mjs` and the two cars in `race-telemetry-story.mjs`, draw the pictures
again and rebuild.
