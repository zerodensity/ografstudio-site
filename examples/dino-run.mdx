A cartoon, not a broadcast graphic: fifteen seconds in the spirit of an endless runner, built to
show how far a timeline can go. An orange dinosaur in a Viking helmet runs to the right, jumps a
rock and ducks under a dragonfly. It catches an old blue dinosaur walking on a cane, eats it and
grows. Then it runs into a wall. The camera pulls back, and the wall turns out to be the first
letter of a very large word.

There is no video in it and no script that runs while it plays. Every move is a key on Studio's
timeline. All the pictures are original pixel art drawn for this example, and all the sounds were
made for it.

## Try it

The cartoon plays when the page opens. A browser keeps a page silent until you press something on
it, so that first run is usually without sound. The sound comes on with your first press, anywhere
on the page.

1. Press **Replay**, at any moment. The cartoon starts over, with its 23 sound effects, and holds
   its last picture.
2. Change **Time of day** to Day while it plays. The sky, the light on the land and the colour of
   the big word change in place, and the cartoon plays on.
3. Change **Landscape** to City. The desert gives way to a skyline and a road.
4. Pick new colours for the three animals. Their shading and their costumes stay as they are.
5. Press **Take out** while it plays. The cartoon stops where it is and fades away, and **Take in**
   starts it again from its first frame.
6. Press **Fullscreen**. The picture is 1920 × 1080 at 50 frames a second.

## What you see

- **The run:** the Viking dinosaur, with a V on its side, jumps a rock and ducks under a dragonfly.
- **The meal:** an old blue dinosaur, with a C on its side, walks slowly on a cane in a cowboy hat.
  It is eaten, and only the hat is left behind. The Viking dinosaur grows.
- **The wall:** bigger now, it smashes through a rock, jumps the lava and runs into a wall. Stars
  go round its head.
- **The pull-back:** the camera pulls back until the whole word is in the picture. A pterodactyl in
  a trapper hat, with an X on its wing, has flown along all the while. It reaches the wall a moment
  earlier, and its beak is stuck in the letter.
- **The meteor:** as the camera pulls back, a meteor comes into the sky. It is still on its way when
  everything stops.

## What you get

- **One long Take in:** the whole cartoon is the way in, 750 frames of it, and its first frame
  already holds the whole scene. The graphic then holds its last frame. **Take out** fades it away,
  in the middle of the cartoon too, and taken in again it plays from the first frame.
- **Sprites by opacity:** each pose of an animal is its own layer, and all of an animal's poses
  share the same position keys. A pose changes by one layer's opacity going to nothing while the
  next one's comes up, inside a single frame.
- **A world that scrolls:** rocks, lava and the old dinosaur ride on position keys. The hills,
  buildings, pebbles and road marks are strips that repeat.
- **A camera made of keys:** there is no camera in Studio. The pull-back is a position key and a size
  key on everything that stands in the world, all with the same easing. The big word is one vector
  shape, drawn seven times larger in the close view.
- **Colours you can change:** each pose is a vector shape under a picture. The shape takes the
  colour field, and the picture on top holds the outline, the shading and the costume.
- **Value maps:** **Time of day** picks a night or a day picture for each strip, and the colours of
  the sky and the word, through value maps on those layers.
- **Rules:** the desert and the city are separate layers. A rule on each shows or hides it by the
  **Landscape** field.
- **Sound cues:** 23 sound effects sit on the timeline as sound cues, each at its frame: the jumps
  and landings, the bite, the growing, the hit on the wall, the meteor's rumble and a short tune at
  the end. Sound makes this a real-time graphic only.

## Make it yours

Download the `.ogs` file above and open it in Studio with **File → Open**. The pixel art, the
sounds and the timing come from the scripts in the site's
[`examples-src/dino-run`](https://github.com/zerodensity/ografstudio-site/tree/main/examples-src/dino-run)
folder: the animals are grids of characters in `art.mjs`, the sounds are a few lines of arithmetic
each in `sound.mjs`, and `build.mjs` places every layer and key. The README in `examples-src` says
how to rebuild it.
