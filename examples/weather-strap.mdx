A strap for the weather in a news bulletin: one city, from today's real forecast. **It's live:** your
browser asked Open-Meteo for it when this page opened, and asks again every quarter of an hour, so the
numbers are the ones forecast right now. When it's raining or snowing there, the weather falls inside
the icon box.

## Try it

1. Pick a **City** and **When**: now, today, tonight or tomorrow. The number rolls to the new value and
   the words change in place.
2. Press **Take out**, then **Take in**. The icon box rises first and the bar wipes open behind it.

## What you get

- **Live data:** the page asks Open-Meteo for the forecast and sends it to the graphic, then again
  every quarter of an hour.
- **Functions:** one function, Strap, writes the words, the number, the high and low and the icon for
  the city and time chosen. The icon moves by itself: rain falls and clouds drift inside its picture.
- **A see-through shader:** the same rain, snow, thunder and fog as the
  [Europe weather](/examples/weather) map, filling the icon box.
- **Data updates:** the numbers roll up to their new values.

## How the data gets there

| Field | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `cities` | The same forecast as Europe weather: one entry per city |
| `city` | Which city the strap is about |
| `when` | `now`, `today`, `tonight` or `tomorrow` |
| `credit` | Where the forecast comes from, under the strap |

The forecast comes from [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/), and your browser asks for it directly
while the graphic is on screen.
