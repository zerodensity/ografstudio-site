The small channel identifier, or digital on-screen graphic (DOG), that stays in a corner of the
screen.

## Try it

Above the canvas, open **+ Recipe…** and choose **Bug / DOG**.

## What you get

- **Layers:** a textured panel, an accent bar and a label, placed inside the safe area.
- **Fields:** channel and live indicator.
- **Motion:** a short entrance and exit.

## Make it yours

- Replace the label with your logo using **Add Image**, and keep the panel as its backing.
- Stack the [clock](/examples/clock) and [countdown](/examples/countdown) recipes underneath; they
  share the same corner layout.
- Bind the label to a **Select** field to switch between programme names.
