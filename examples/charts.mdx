A set of nine templates draws the same four teams as horizontal and vertical bars, a stacked bar,
a line, an area, a pie, a doughnut, a radar and a polar area. The values are shared controls, so
one number changes every template in the set. All nine play on this page: the bar above, then the
other eight in the order they sit in the set. The download above is the whole set.

Bars grow to the right, staggered from the top row down, with room beyond the longest bar for its
value.

## Try it

- Change a team's value in any player. The mark and its readout move together, because both read
  the same field.
- Download the `.ogs` from the first player's caption and open it in Studio. The **Templates** tab
  lists all nine; select one and click **Take In** in **Preview & Export**.
- [Make your first chart](/guides/first-chart) builds the bar from a blank template, then uses the
  set to try the prepared motion.

## What you get

- **Layers:** a Chart layer, a title block, a readout for each team and a mask for the exit. All of
  them are ordinary layers.
- **Fields:** headline, subtitle and four integers, one per team. Across the set they are shared
  controls, so a value set once applies to all nine templates.
- **Motion:** a 2-second In and a 1-second Out. Bars grow from the baseline with a stagger, lines
  and areas trace left to right, and the circular charts sweep clockwise from 12 o'clock.

## Make it yours

- Rename the teams in the chart's rows in **Properties → Chart** and in the readout layers.
- In the circular templates, change the numbers and leave the masks and bindings as they are.
- The templates in the set have an opaque navy background. The players here were exported with a
  transparent one, so the backdrop swatches show through; set **Background** to **Transparent** in
  the composition if you want the same.
- Apply your Brand Kit for colours and type, so the set matches the rest of your package.
