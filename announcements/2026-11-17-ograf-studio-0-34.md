OGraf Studio 0.34 is out. Others on your local network can now watch the graphic you are working on,
live, in their own browser, while you keep editing in Studio.

<!-- truncate -->

## Share the Live preview

Start Studio as usual. The server prints a viewer address, with a QR code in a color terminal, and
anyone on your network can open it in a browser or scan it with a phone. When you open a template or
a coordinated template set, the viewer shows its Live preview, and it follows the next template you
open at the same address.

With **Sync viewers** checked, every viewer follows your playout, your pointer and your mouse and
keyboard actions in the graphic. Uncheck it to give each viewer their own playout and data controls,
and check it again to bring everyone back to your output. The separate **Operator link** opens the
same controls on another computer. Viewers that reconnect pick up the current state, and the preview
keeps running while the Studio server runs, even after you close the editor tab.

The editor and the connection for AI agents still accept connections only from your own computer.
Anyone who can reach the viewer address can watch the preview and fetch its playback files, so use it
on a network you trust. Start the server with `--no-playout` to turn the viewer off, or choose its
address with `--playout-host` and `--playout-port`.
[View Studio's Live preview on the LAN](/docs/development#view-studios-live-preview-on-the-lan) has
the details.

## Fixes

- Taking a graphic out from its final Step plays the whole **Out** animation you keyed, in live
  playback and in rendering. An early or interrupted Out still starts from the current frame.
- **Arabic-script text animation** keeps letters joined, keeps combining marks in place and keeps
  mixed text in the right order, including Urdu, in Studio and in exports.
- Masks on **Auto Size** text follow the text as its content or font size changes.
- The bundled Chart.js is now 4.6.1.
